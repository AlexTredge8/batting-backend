# Batting IQ: Alex's goals and restrictions (the brief every agent works from)

Agreed in conversation, 16 Nov 2026. Read this before starting any task.

## Goals
1. **Tips first.** The main fault and the tip must point the right way. The score only has to be roughly right, with clips in the right order.
2. **Match real coaches** as closely as possible.
3. **Progress with what we have.** Use the existing clips and Alex checking the AI's answers. Do not wait for net sessions.
4. **An AI that learns from corrections.** It suggests, Alex (or Cam) ticks or corrects, the app's own model retrains, and agreement is measured each round.
5. **Clips we may lawfully use commercially.** Main route: users opt in at upload, with parent consent for under-18s. Also: rewards for shared clips, licences from coaches or clubs, Cam's consented sessions, and the CricRAG authors.

## Restrictions
1. **Little of Alex's time.** Short tap-through tasks. One Terminal command at a time. No filming by Alex.
2. **No ongoing AI costs.** Paid AI such as Gemini is used only while developing, as a one-off and within a set budget (GBP 3.60 left on 16 Nov). It never runs in the live app on every upload. Ask before going over budget.
3. **Privacy.** Clips stay private. Faces are blurred whenever clips are shown outside the team. Nothing is scraped from YouTube or the web, and no research-only datasets are used. Only consented uploads are used (see docs/legal/training_data_rules.md).
4. **Nothing goes live untested.** A change goes live only when it is tested, is not worse than today, and Alex has been told first.
5. **Plain English, UK spelling.** Every request to Alex or Cam says exactly what to do and why, in lay terms.

## People
- **Alex:** owner and qualified coach. Validates AI answers, signs off the checklist, runs Mac commands.
- **Cam:** qualified coach. Second opinion: signs off the checklist, rates clips, and may film a session later (optional).

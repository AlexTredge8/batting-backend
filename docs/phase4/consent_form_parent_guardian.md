# Consent forms: filming for Batting IQ app testing

> **Note for Alex (delete this box before you print).**
> - This is a template. It is not legal advice.
> - Before you use it, check it with the club's safeguarding or welfare officer. The welfare officer must know about the session, its date and who will film.
> - Follow the club's own photography and filming policy, and the ECB's Safe Hands guidance on photos and filming.
> - Check whether Batting IQ must pay the ICO data protection fee (ico.org.uk). Consider a short data protection impact assessment (DPIA), because the data is about children.
> - Fill in the email line and the session date before you print.
> - Keep the signed forms apart from the clips. Keep a list of anyone who withdraws.
> - There are two versions below: **Form A** for players under 18, and **Form B** for adult players.

## Form A: players under 18 (parent or guardian consent, and player assent)

### About this filming

**Who we are.** Batting IQ is a UK app that gives feedback on a cricket front-foot drive from a phone video. Contact: Alex Tredgett, Batting IQ. Email: ____________________________

**What we film.** On ______________ (date), the coach, Cam, will film your child playing 2–4 front-foot drives in the nets. The phone stands behind the bowler, so it mostly sees the batter's body and bat from the front, at a distance. We do not film faces close up. We record each player under a code (for example P07), not a name. Only Cam keeps the list that links codes to names.

**Why we film.** To test and improve how the app judges batting technique. Qualified coaches watch the clips and rate the technique. We then check whether the app's scores agree with the coaches. We may also use measurements from the clips to tune the app's scoring. We do not use the clips to recognise who anyone is (no face recognition).

**Where we keep the clips.** In private cloud storage with a password and two-step sign-in. Only the Batting IQ team and named coaches can open it. We do not send clips by WhatsApp or post them online.

**Faces blurred.** If we show a clip to anyone outside that group, we blur the face first.

**No publicity.** We will not publish the clips or use them in marketing, adverts or social media. If we ever want to, we will ask you again, separately. You can say no.

**How long we keep them.** We delete the clips within 24 months of the session date, or sooner if you ask.

**It is your choice.** Taking part is optional. Saying no, or withdrawing later, does not affect your child's coaching or team selection in any way.

**Withdrawing.** You or your child can withdraw at any time, before or after filming. To withdraw, email Alex at the address above, or tell Cam. Give the child's name and the session date. We then delete your child's clips and the measurements made from them within one month. Results already worked out from many clips together (for example, an average) cannot be undone, but they do not show or identify your child.

**The law.** Under UK GDPR, our lawful basis is your consent. You can ask to see the clips we hold, ask us to correct or delete them, or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113). We follow the spirit of the ICO Children's Code: we collect only what we need, keep it only as long as we need it, and put your child's best interests first.

# Filming guide for Cam: one session, one lane, all levels

**Why this session matters.** In our 18 test clips, every good batter was filmed in one net lane and every weaker batter somewhere else. So the app can "learn" the venue instead of the technique. This session fixes that: good and weak batters, filmed in the same lane, with the same phone set-up.

**Target:** 30–60 clips from 15–30 players. Include all levels, from beginners to your best players. Try to get at least 4 players at each level.

## Before the session

1. Tell the club welfare officer about the session.
2. Get a signed consent form for every player you will film (`consent_form_parent_guardian.md`). Under-18s need a parent or guardian signature.
3. Do not film a player who has no signed form. Do not film a player who says no on the day, even with a form.
4. Turn off location tagging for the camera app.
5. Make a names list on paper or in your phone notes: player code (P01, P02 …) next to each name. This list stays with you. Do not send it to anyone.

## Set up the phone (once, for everyone)

1. Use the back camera. Do not use the selfie camera.
2. Set the video to 1080p. Do not use 4K. Do not use slow motion. Use 60 fps if the phone offers it easily; otherwise use 30 fps.
3. Put the phone on a tripod, or wedge it so it cannot move.
4. Put the phone at chest height, directly behind the bowler's stumps (or directly behind the bowling machine).
5. Turn on the grid. Make the phone level.
6. Zoom so that the batter fills about one third of the picture height.
7. Choose landscape or portrait. Use the same choice for all players.
8. Do not move the phone, the zoom or the lane for the rest of the session. If the phone is knocked, put it back in the same position and write down the time.

## Film each player

1. Give each player 2–4 full-length balls to drive: off side, straight and on side.
2. Start recording before the bowler runs in (or before the machine feeds the ball).
3. Stop recording after the follow-through.
4. Record one drive in each clip, if possible.
5. Mix the levels through the session. Do not film all the best players first and all the beginners last.

## Name each clip

Use this format: `player code _ ball number _ hand _ level`.

- Hand: `R` (right-handed) or `L` (left-handed).
- Level, as you judge it: `Beginner`, `Average`, `GoodClub` or `Elite`.
- Example: `P07_03_R_Average.mov` (player 7, third ball, right-handed, average).

Keep a session sheet for Alex. On it, write for each player: code, hand, level, and **bowling machine or bowler**. Do not write names on this sheet.

## Upload

1. Upload the clips and the session sheet to the private folder that Alex shares with you (OneDrive or Google Drive).
2. Do not send clips on WhatsApp. WhatsApp compresses video below 720p, and the app cannot use it.
3. Do not send clips by email or post them anywhere.
4. When Alex confirms that the upload is complete, delete the clips from your phone.
5. Keep the signed consent forms in a safe place. Do not put them in the clips folder.

## Do not

- Do not film faces close up.
- Do not film other children in the foreground. Check the picture before each player.
- Do not film anyone who has no signed form.
- Do not film in changing rooms or away from the net lane.
- Do not share clips with players, parents or social media.

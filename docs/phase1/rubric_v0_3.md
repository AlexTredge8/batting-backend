C14 (Cam: "top half closes") keeps its ID but is rated inside C07, so the same idea is not rated twice.

**Do not mark down (this camera cannot see it):** stride length and depth; head forward of the base; knee ahead of the toes; how far in front of the eyes the ball is met; bat face; timing and where the ball went.

**Then:** score the four pillars (0–25) and the overall (0–100) on its own. Pick ONE priority fault, usually the earliest in the chain. If the ball was too short to drive (C19 = 0), give F19; do not blame the technique.

## What changed from v0.2 and why

| Item | Change | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| C03 | A backlift towards the slips is no longer a fault | Most international batters lift that way (Noorbhai & Noakes 2019). Only a bat that comes down across the line matters, and C08 rates that | research, Radford |
| C05 | Landing just inside the line counts as done well | Elite batters often land just inside the line (Stuelcken 2005); the manuals say "to the pitch of the ball" | consensus, research |
| C07 | "Side-on until contact" becomes "front shoulder aligned to the shot until contact", judged from the stride on | No manual asks for side-on until contact; Radford wants a square-on, ready position at release | consensus, Radford |
| C14 | Rated as part of C07 | Same idea as the new C07 (close the top half, then keep it there); not found in the manuals; keeps the list at 18 | consensus (Cam to confirm) |
| C09 | A brief settle of the front foot is not a hitch | CSA coaches the settle; skilled batters land as the downswing starts (Weissensteiner 2011) | consensus, research |
| C10 | Adds "ball met close to the front pad" and "still" | NZC, Namibia and ICC Europe all coach it; the sideways gap is visible. Replaces a separate contact check | consensus, Radford |
| C12 | A short, controlled finish is fine | The manuals accept both checked and full finishes | consensus |
| C17 | New: head position as the bowler lets go | Radford checks where the head is, not only whether it is still. Measured by M15 | Radford |
| C18 | New: back foot drives off | NZC and Discovery Vitality coach it; v0.2 had nothing on the back leg | consensus |
| C19 | New: shot choice | Every source treats the drive as the shot for a full ball | consensus |
| Eyes level | No new ID | Already part of C01 | consensus |
| Order | Checkpoints and faults in chain order, set-up to finish | The priority fault is usually the earliest one | rubric rule |
| Tips | New `fault_tips_v0_3.json`; F08 tip now names the usual cause (stance and eye line) | Radford links the across-the-line swing to the set-up | Radford |

**Faults and fixes he gives.**
- Eyes outside off stump and knees ahead of the toes leads to falling to the off side and the bat coming across. The fix is to sit into the stance, head over toes, eyes inside off.
- A static or back-pocket backlift is fixed by hands below the head, going back and up.
- A head drifting off side at release is fixed by moving the front foot across in the stance and standing more side-on.
- A stiff front leg is fixed by taking the weight through a bent front knee.

**What this means for the rubric (for Cam to confirm).**
1. Add a head-position check at release. Do not just check stillness. It is visible from this camera, and the app can measure the head's sideways position against the feet.
2. Combine C03 and C13 into one backlift check: hands close and below the head, top hand leading, bat already rising before the downswing.
3. In the C08 tip, point to the cause, set-up and eye line, not just the across-the-line swing.
4. Add "still and close to the head at contact" to C10.
5. Judge C07 from the stride onwards.

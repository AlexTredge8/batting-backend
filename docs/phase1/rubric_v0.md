Scope: Front-foot drive, controlled along the ground, filmed from the bowler's end. Score this shot, not the player. Source of truth: `rubric_v0.json` (this page is generated from it).

## Rate each checkpoint

2 = done well · 1 = partly, or a small fault · 0 = clear fault · X = cannot judge from this clip (say why)

| # | Stage | Checkpoint | Pillar | Visible from the bowler's end |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | Set-up | Head level and still at set-up; eyes level | Tracking | yes |
| C02 | Set-up | Balanced base at set-up; weight even, knees relaxed | Stability | partly: stance width lies along the pitch |
| C03 | Backlift | Hands lift straight back, not out towards gully or across the body | Access | yes for hands; the bat is not tracked |
| C04 | Movement | Head and front shoulder lead towards the line of the ball | Tracking | yes for line; no for forward movement |
| C05 | Movement | Front foot lands close to the line of the ball | Access | yes |
| C06 | Movement | Front knee bends and takes the weight | Stability | partly: foreshortened |
| C07 | Movement | Body stays side-on until contact; chest does not open early | Access | yes: the shoulder line widens as it opens |
| C08 | Downswing | Downswing comes straight through the line of the ball | Access | partly: hands yes, bat no |
| C09 | Downswing | One rhythm from backlift to contact; no pause or hitch | Flow | yes, limited by frame rate |
| C10 | Contact | Head over the ball at contact; does not fall away | Tracking | sideways yes; forward no |
| C11 | Finish | Balance held at the finish; no fall to the off or leg side | Stability | yes for sideways falls |
| C12 | Finish | Hands continue through towards the target; finish controlled, not checked or wild | Flow | yes |

**Cannot be judged from this camera; do not mark these down:** Stride length and weight forward (depth along the pitch); Head forward of the base; Contact under the eyes, beside the front pad; Bat face; Timing and where the ball went.

## Then score the pillars (0–25) and the overall (0–100)

Score the overall on its own. Do not add up the pillars.

| Pillar | About 5 | About 15 | About 25 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Access | Plays across the line: foot well away from the line, chest open early, hands out wide | Gets near the line, but opens a little early or the hands drift | Foot to the line, side-on to contact, hands straight through the line |
| Tracking | Head moves early and falls away at contact | Head mostly still, leads late, drifts a little at contact | Still, level head that leads to the line and stays over the ball |
| Stability | Unbalanced from set-up, falls away at the finish | Balanced, but the weight stays back or the finish wobbles | Balanced base, weight onto a bent front knee, finish held |
| Flow | Stop-start: pause at the top, hitch, checked finish | Mostly connected, with one hesitation | One smooth rhythm from backlift to a full, controlled finish |

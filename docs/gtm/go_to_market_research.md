# How AI coaching apps make money, and a route to market for Batting IQ (research, 15 Nov 2026)

These figures come from search-result snippets, because the session could not fetch outside pages. Many come from third-party trackers. Check prices in the live app stores before using them.

## GolfFix: what it earns and what it's worth

Neither figure is public. MOAIS, a private company in Seoul, owns it.
- **Funding:** about ₩8bn+ in disclosed equity, roughly £4–5m. The rounds were:
  - seed in 2021 (TheVentures, Kimgisa Lab);
  - Series A, ₩2.5bn, in 2022;
  - Series B, ₩5.5bn (about £3m), in April 2026, led by Yuanta.
- **Users:** about 1.5m cumulative downloads, and roughly 100k monthly active users on Android (tracker estimate).
- **Price:** free with ads; Advanced plan $99.99 or £99.99 a year.
- **Other products:** MOAIS has added a golf-simulator kiosk and an enterprise product, so consumer subscriptions are probably not its whole income.
- **Rough estimate, illustration only:** if 3–5% of 100k active users pay about $70 net a year, consumer revenue is under $1m a year.

## Recommendations

1. **Price:**
   - Free: 3 analyses a month, with the full score and storyboard.
   - Premium: £6.99 a month or £39.99 a year, with annual as the default.
   - Season Pass: £24.99, April to September.
   - Why: cricketers pay less than golfers (CricHeroes £29.99 a year), and a UK lesson costs £25–70.
2. **Trial:** 14–30 days, timed for winter indoor nets (January–March). Longer trials convert better: median 45.7% against 26.8%.
3. **First channel: coaches.**
   - Give the first 100 qualified coaches free accounts.
   - Then offer a Coach tier at about £14.99 a month for 25 players.
   - Precedents: SwingVision with LTA coaches; Sportsbox and V1 both build on coaches.
4. **Clubs and academies:** a club pack at £249–399 a year that clubs can fold into junior subs (the PitchVision model). The ECB lists 5,700 clubs.
5. **Governing bodies:** start talking to county boards now, using the LTA–SwingVision deal as the template. Don't depend on this in year 1.
6. **Ambassadors:** county and women's players, and coaching YouTubers, on revenue share. No star players yet.
7. **Shareable output:** a vertical storyboard clip with the score, a watermark and a referral code worth a free analysis. Measure the share rate.
8. **App-store search before ads:** target terms like "cricket batting coach" and "cricket shot analysis". The niche looks uncontested; CricVision has about 5k installs.
9. **No ads in the free tier.** GolfFix's bad reviews are about ads; Fulltrack's are about price.
10. **No hardware.**
11. **No broad paid acquisition yet.** One app install costs up to $26.70 in US Sports, against about £34 net a year from each paying subscriber at £39.99. A £500 Meta test in April is enough.
12. **UK first.** Use the UK South Asian diaspora as a bridge market. Leave India (price ceiling about ₹99 a month) and Australia (about 670k players) until later.

## Risks

1. **Small, seasonal market that pays little.** About 1.2m recreational players in England and Wales. Incumbents such as CricHeroes or Fulltrack could add an "AI tip" feature.
2. **Accuracy and retention.** Failed detections or generic tips lead to one-star reviews and cancellations. Monthly plans may keep only about 7% of subscribers after a year.
3. **Junior safeguarding.** UK GDPR, the ICO Children's Code and parental consent must be designed in before the club channel opens.

## Market data

- **England and Wales:** 1.2m+ recreational players, 5,700 clubs and 43,000+ teams.
- **Australia:** 669,642 registered participants (2024–25).
- **UK one-to-one coaching:** £25–70 an hour.

# Drill and video sign-off sheet (for Alex)

**Who:** Alex (qualified coach). Cam is not asked to do this; Cam's job is the checkpoint rating only.
**Why:** the consumer and safeguarding review (Issue 4) says a qualified coach must check every drill before players see it. Your sign-off is our evidence that we took reasonable care. Nothing goes live until it is done.
**Time:** about 40 minutes for the drills (part 1), plus the time to watch the videos (part 2). You can do it in several sittings.

## How to do it

1. Open `docs/phase6/drill_library.md` (the drills in full, in chain order) next to this sheet.
2. For each drill, read the steps, the safety line and the check. Ask yourself: is it right for the fault, safe for a junior with an adult supervising, and at the right level?
3. Tick **OK**, or tick **Change** and write the change in a few words (for example "F05-C: throw from 6 m, not 8").
4. For part 2, open each video link. Watch enough to check: it matches the fault, the advice agrees with the rubric (for example, a bat lifting towards the slips is fine), and nothing in it or its suggested videos is unsuitable for juniors. Tick **OK** or **Not suitable**. For a long video, write the start time of the useful part.
5. Send your answers back in one message (template at the end). We then set `signoff` in `drill_library.json` and `content_reviewed` in `drill_videos.json`, and update the app with the follow-up templates in `docs/lovable/drill_plan_prompt.md`.

Things that are already fixed for every drill, so you need not check them one by one: every partner drill uses a tennis ball; nobody stands in line with the shot or within reach of the bat; every card also shows the general safety line and, for under-18s or unknown age, the adult-supervision and helmet-with-faceguard line; no drill claims to prevent injury.

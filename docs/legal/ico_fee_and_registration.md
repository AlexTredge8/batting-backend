## Why Batting IQ must pay

Every organisation or sole trader that processes personal data must pay the fee unless exempt (Data Protection (Charges and Information) Regulations 2018, as amended in 2025: UNVERIFIED citation; the 2025 amending regulations came into force on 17 February 2025: UNVERIFIED (snippet): S1).

**The exemptions** cover processing **only** for (UNVERIFIED (snippet): S30):
- staff administration;
- advertising, marketing and public relations for your own business;
- accounts and records;
- some not-for-profit purposes;
- personal, family or household purposes;
- (and a few others: for example judicial functions, or processing not done on a computer: UNVERIFIED).

Batting IQ processes customers' videos and account details to **provide its service** and to **train its model**. That is outside every exemption. **Not exempt.**

## What happens if you don't pay

The ICO can issue a fixed penalty: **£400** for tier 1 (£600 tier 2, £4,000 tier 3), up to **£4,350** with aggravating factors (UNVERIFIED (snippet): S2; these figures date from 2018–19 and may have changed). The ICO also publishes a register of fee payers, so non-payment is easy to spot.

## Who registers: decide this first

The fee is paid by the **legal person** that controls the data:
- **If Batting IQ is not a company:** Alex registers as a sole trader, for example "Alex Tredgett, trading as Batting IQ".
- **If Batting IQ Ltd exists:** the company registers, with its Companies House number.
- **If you incorporate later:** the company needs its own registration; cancel the sole-trader one at the same time (UNVERIFIED that a new registration is needed rather than an update, but a change of legal person is normally a new controller).
- **One fee covers everything that person does.** If Alex also coaches for money as the same sole trader, the one fee covers both (UNVERIFIED).

The ICO publishes the controller's name and address on its public register (UNVERIFIED that the address is shown in full). If you are a sole trader working from home, consider using a business or correspondence address you are happy to see published.

## 7. Safety

7.1 Cricket with a hard ball can cause serious injury. Whenever you practise:
- warm up first;
- make sure there is enough space, and that nobody is within reach of your swing;
- use a tennis ball or soft ball for partner drills, with the feeder standing to the side, not in line with the shot;
- with a hard ball, always wear a helmet with a faceguard and proper protective equipment;
- stop if anything hurts.

## 1. Why we have this policy

Batting IQ is a UK app that gives feedback on a cricket front-foot drive from a phone video. Some of our users and some of the players in our research sessions are under 18.

This policy sets out how we keep children safe when:
- children's videos are uploaded to the app;
- we film sessions; and
- coaches review videos.

It applies to everyone who works with Batting IQ: the owner, coaches who help us (including Cam), contractors, and any club, coach or ambassador working with us.

**Principles:**
- The welfare of the child comes first.
- Every child has a right to be safe, whatever their age, disability, gender, race, religion or belief, or sexual orientation.
- We follow the **ECB Safe Hands policy** (cricket's policy for safeguarding children) and the safeguarding policy of any club we work with. Where they set a higher standard than this policy, theirs applies.
- We follow **NSPCC Sport** guidance on photographing and filming children in sport.

## 3. Consent

- **Filming sessions:**
  - written consent from a parent or guardian for every player under 18, using our consent form (Form A);
  - the player's own agreement (age 12 and over in writing; under 12 asked in person);
  - adults sign Form B.
- **No form, no filming.** If a child says no on the day, we do not film them, even with a signed form.
- **App uploads:** using a video of an under-18 to train our AI, or linking an under-18 to a coach, needs the parent or guardian's own agreement. It is never assumed and never pre-ticked.
- **No pressure.** We never offer rewards to under-18s for sharing videos, and we do not nudge them to agree.
- **Withdrawal at any time:** by the child or the parent, without giving a reason. Withdrawal never affects a child's coaching or team selection.

## 4. Filming sessions

1. **Approval first.** The club welfare officer (or another club official) approves the session in writing: date, place, who will film and why. We follow the club's photography and filming policy.
2. **Notice on the day** at the nets, saying filming is taking place and who to speak to.
3. **Two adults.** At least two adults are present whenever under-18s are filmed, and filming happens in open view, in the net lane only. Parents are welcome to watch.
4. **What we film.** Only batting in the nets, from behind the bowler, at a distance. No close-ups of faces. No filming in changing rooms or away from the nets. Players in normal cricket kit.
5. **Bystanders.** We check the picture before each player so that no child without consent is in shot.
6. **Codes, not names.** Clips are labelled with a player code (e.g. P07). The list linking codes to names is kept on paper with the consent forms, never with the clips and never in a phone or cloud note.

## 5. Storage and keeping

- **Where:** private, password-protected cloud storage with two-step sign-in. Only the Batting IQ team and named coaches who have agreed in writing to keep clips confidential can open it. No public links.
- **Never by WhatsApp, text, email or social media.** We never send clips on messaging apps or post them anywhere.
- **Phones:**
  - turn off automatic photo backup before filming;
  - upload clips to the private folder the same day;
  - then delete them from the phone, empty "Recently deleted", and check the phone's cloud photo library.
- **Consent forms:** kept securely (locked or encrypted), apart from the clips, for as long as we keep the clips plus [1 year].
- **How long:**
  - session clips: deleted within **24 months** of the session, or sooner on request;
  - app videos: deleted as set out in our Privacy Notice.
- **Withdrawal:** we delete a child's clips and the measurements made from them within one month of being asked.

## 6. Face blurring and sharing

- **Outside the team, faces are blurred.** Whenever a video or still is shown to anyone outside the Batting IQ team (including coaches using our rating tools, reports and presentations), the face is blurred first.
- **We do not publish.** We never publish children's videos or images, or use them in marketing or social media.
- **Names and images are never shown together.** We do not show a child's name with their image.
- **Share cards made by young users:**
  - never show their name;
  - always blur the face;
  - never include a referral code;
  - remind them to ask a parent before sharing.
- **No face recognition.** We do not use videos to recognise or identify anyone.

## 7. Coach and club access in the app

*[Applies once coach and club accounts exist.]*

- A coach can see an under-18 player's videos only if:
  - the player's parent or guardian has approved the link; and
  - the club has confirmed the coach is appointed and vetted.
- Coaches cannot download or share players' videos. Every view is logged.
- No private messaging between coaches and under-18s in the app. Contact about Batting IQ goes through parents or the club's approved channels, in line with ECB guidance on messaging and social media.
- Clubs remove a coach's access as soon as they leave.

## 8. Communicating with young people

- We contact parents or guardians, not under-18s directly, about consent, sessions and results.
- We do not add under-18s to WhatsApp groups or follow or message them on social media.
- Ambassadors working with us are adults. They do not film or post about children for Batting IQ.

## 9. Reporting a concern

**If a child is in immediate danger, call 999.**

A concern could be:
- something a child says;
- an inappropriate video or image uploaded to the app;
- a coach or adult behaving inappropriately;
- a clip that has been shared without permission; or
- a data breach involving children's videos.

1. **Do not investigate yourself.** Listen, reassure the child, do not promise to keep secrets, and write down what was said and seen, with the date and time, as soon as possible.
2. **Tell the Batting IQ Safeguarding Lead** the same day: [safeguarding email], [phone].
3. **The Safeguarding Lead tells the club welfare officer** the same day. If they are unavailable or involved, the lead tells the county safeguarding officer or the ECB Safeguarding Team.
4. **Concerns about an adult** who works with children (e.g. a coach) are also reported through the club welfare officer and the ECB to the Local Authority Designated Officer (LADO), as the ECB process requires (UNVERIFIED: confirm the current ECB route with the welfare officer).
5. **Inappropriate or illegal images:**
   - do not copy, forward or download them;
   - restrict access in the app at once;
   - report to the police, and through the club or ECB route.
6. **Data breaches** involving children's videos are also handled under our data protection procedures, including reporting to the ICO where required (see `dp_review.md`).
7. **Advice:** the NSPCC helpline gives free advice to adults worried about a child: [0808 800 5000] (UNVERIFIED: check the current number on nspcc.org.uk).

Records of concerns are kept securely, separately from other records, and shared only with those who need to know.

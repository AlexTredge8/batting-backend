# Draft wording: using uploaded videos to train and test Batting IQ

> **Read this first.**
> - **This is a template, not legal advice.** A UK solicitor who knows data protection must review it before launch. The questions for them are listed at the end.
> - **ICO fee.** Check whether Batting IQ must register with the ICO and pay the data protection fee. Most businesses that handle personal data on computers must. It is a short online form at ico.org.uk; at the time of writing the smallest tier is £52 a year, but check the current amount. Put the registration number in the notice below.
> - **DPIA.** Do a short data protection impact assessment before launch, because the app handles children's videos. The ICO's Children's code expects one for online services that children are likely to use. The ICO publishes a template, and a few pages is enough at this size.

## (a) Terms of Service clause

**[X]. Using your videos to improve Batting IQ (optional)**

**[X].1 What you always give us.** When you upload a video, you let us store it and analyse it so that we can give you your score, tips and storyboard. We need this to provide the service. It does not include training our coaching AI.

**[X].2 What you can choose to give us.** At upload, you can choose to let us use a video to train and test Batting IQ's coaching AI. This is optional, and the box is unticked unless you tick it. If you say yes for a video, you give us a non-exclusive, royalty-free permission to copy, store and analyse that video, and to make measurements from it, only to:
- (a) train, test and improve Batting IQ's analysis of batting technique;
- (b) let qualified cricket coaches who work with us watch it and rate the technique; and
- (c) check that our scores and tips agree with those coaches.

**[X].3 What we will not do.** We will not:
- sell your video;
- licence it to anyone else for their own use;
- publish it;
- use it in adverts, marketing or social media;
- use it to recognise who anyone is.

We will only do any of these if we ask you separately and you agree. If we show it to anyone outside the Batting IQ team, we blur the face first.

**[X].4 Saying no or changing your mind.** Saying no does not change your analysis or anything else in the app. You can withdraw your permission for any video at any time in Account, under "My videos and data", or by emailing [Privacy email]. When you do:
- we stop using the video for anything new;
- we remove it, and the measurements made from it, from our training and test sets within 30 days.

Training already done can't be undone, but our coaching AI does not contain or show your video. Withdrawing does not affect anything we lawfully did before you withdrew.

**[X].5 Players under 18.** If the batter in the video is under 18, only their parent or guardian can give this permission. The player, or the parent or guardian, can withdraw it at any time.

**[X].6 Your promises.** When you give this permission, you confirm that:
- you are the batter in the video, or the batter (or, if they are under 18, their parent or guardian) has agreed;
- you have the right to share the video: you filmed it, or the person who filmed it agrees;
- if other people can be seen clearly in the video, you have taken reasonable care that they do not mind. If you are unsure, do not tick the box.

**[X].7 Who owns what.** You keep any rights you have in your video. We own the improvements we make to Batting IQ, including our coaching AI. Our coaching AI does not include copies of your video.

**[X].8 How long we keep it.** We keep a video you have shared for training for up to 3 years from the date you agreed, or until you withdraw or delete it, if that is sooner. Our Privacy Notice explains more.

**[X].9 Rewards (only if we run an offer).** From time to time we may offer a reward for sharing videos for training, for example a free month of Premium.
- Rewards are only for players aged 18 and over.
- The offer's own terms will say what you need to do.
- If you withdraw your permission later, you keep the reward.
- Not taking part never costs you anything.

**[X].10 Removing videos.** We may remove any video from our training sets at any time, for example if it is not suitable or if we are told the batter did not agree.

## (b) Privacy Notice section

### Using videos to improve Batting IQ (only if you agree)

**Who is responsible.** [Controller], [Address], is the controller of your personal data. ICO registration number: [ICO registration number]. Contact: [Privacy email].

**What we use.** If you agree, for each video you share we use:
- **The video itself.** It shows the batter's body, kit and surroundings, and may show other people in the background.
- **Measurements we make from it.** For example, body positions frame by frame, timings, scores and the faults found.
- **Ratings that qualified coaches give it.**
- **A record of your permission.** This is which video it covers, the version of the wording you saw, the date and time, whether the batter or a parent or guardian agreed, and, for under-18s, the parent or guardian's name and email address.

We do not use your video to recognise who anyone is (no face recognition). We do not use it to work out anything about health, ethnicity or other sensitive matters.

**Why we use it, and our lawful basis.**

| What we do | Lawful basis under UK GDPR |
|---|---|
| Store and analyse your video to give you your score, tips and storyboard | Contract: we need it to provide the service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b)) |
| Use a video to train and test our coaching AI, and have coaches rate it | **Your consent** (Article 6(1)(a)), given separately for each video at upload, or later in your account |
| Keep a record of your permission and of any withdrawal | Legal obligation: we must be able to show that we had consent (Articles 6(1)(c) and 7(1)) |
| Keep the service secure and prevent misuse | Legitimate interests: running a safe service (Article 6(1)(f)) |

Training is separate from the analysis. You can have your analysis without agreeing to training, and saying no changes nothing else.

**Purpose limitation.** We use shared videos only to improve Batting IQ's analysis of batting technique. We will not:
- use them for any other product;
- use them for advertising or marketing;
- profile you;
- sell them, or share them with anyone for their own use.

If we ever want to use them for something new, we will ask you first.

**Data minimisation.**
- We ask a yes or no question about age. We do not ask for a date of birth for this.
- We collect a parent or guardian's name and email only for under-18s, and use them only about this permission.
- Where we can, we work from the measurements rather than the video.
- *[only if true]* We remove location data from videos when they are uploaded.

**Face blurring.** Whenever we show a video, or a still from it, to anyone outside the Batting IQ team, we blur the face first. That includes coaches using our rating tools, and any report or presentation.

**Who can see it, and how we protect it.**
- Videos are kept in private cloud storage. Only the owner and named Batting IQ admins can open them through the app.
- The keys that give wider access are kept only on our servers, never in the app.
- Team accounts use strong passwords and two-step sign-in.
- Copies made for training are kept on encrypted computers. We delete them when you withdraw, or when the video reaches the end of its keeping period.
- Qualified coaches who rate videos agree in writing to keep them confidential. They see them face-blurred, without names.
- We never send videos by messaging apps, and never post them to public links.

**Who helps us (processors).** These companies process data for us under contracts that require them to protect it:

| Company | What it does for us | Where the data may be processed |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase Inc. | Database, video storage and sign-in | [region of the project, for example London (eu-west-2)]; support access from the USA |
| Railway Corporation | Runs our analysis server | [region]; company based in the USA |
| Lovable | Hosts the app's web pages | [check] |
| [Google (Gemini API, paid tier)] | [only if used] One-off checks during development. Its terms must say it does not use our data to train its own models. | [USA and elsewhere] |

We will update this list if it changes.

**International transfers.** Some of these companies are based in, or give support from, the USA and other countries outside the UK. When your data leaves the UK, we rely on one of these:
- the UK-US "data bridge", where the company is certified under it;
- the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum;
- another safeguard allowed by UK law.

You can ask us for details. *[Check each company's certification and contract.]*

**How long we keep it.**
- **A video you shared for training:** up to 3 years from the date you agreed. We delete it sooner if you withdraw (out of our training and test sets within 30 days) or delete it.
- **Measurements and coach ratings made from it:** deleted on the same timetable as the video. Overall results worked out from many videos together (for example, an average or our trained coaching AI) do not identify you, so we keep them.
- **The record of your permission:** kept for as long as the video, plus 1 year, so that we can show we had permission. Then deleted.
- **Videos not shared for training:** [state the app's normal keeping period].
- **Still pictures from your analysis** (storyboard frames): [state the keeping period]. These stay in your history when you delete a video, unless you ask us to delete them too.

**Children.** Many of our users are juniors. We follow the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's code):
- **Best interests first.** We only use a junior's video for training if it helps make Batting IQ better and safer for players, and never in a way that could harm them.
- **High privacy by default.** The training option is off by default, and its box starts unticked on every upload. We never remember a previous "yes".
- **Parental consent.** If the batter is under 18, only a parent or guardian can say yes, with their name, email address and their own tick. We choose 18, stricter than the age of 13 in UK law for some online consent, because the videos are of young people and the use is commercial.
- **No nudging.** We do not use rewards, reminders, pop-ups or highlighted buttons to encourage under-18s to share. Any reward offer is never shown to them. Saying no is as easy as ignoring the option.
- **Clear explanations.** We explain what happens in plain words, with a note asking young players to read it with a parent or guardian.
- **The child's own voice.** A young player can withdraw at any time, even if their parent or guardian agreed.
- **No profiling, no face recognition and no publishing** of children's videos.
- *[only if true]* We email the parent or guardian to confirm their agreement before we use the video.

**Your rights.** You have the right to:
- **withdraw consent** at any time, as easily as you gave it: switch off "Use to improve Batting IQ" in Account, under "My videos and data", or email us;
- **see** the data we hold about you, and get a copy;
- **correct** it;
- **delete** it: use "Delete this video", or email us;
- **restrict** or **object to** how we use it;
- **move** it to another service (data portability).

To use a right, use the account page or email [Privacy email]. It is free, and we reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm who you are.

A parent or guardian can act for a young child. From about age 12, children can usually act for themselves, and we take their wishes into account.

The analysis gives an automatic score and tips. It does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

**Complaints.** Please contact us first at [Privacy email]. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

**Changes.** If we change how we use shared videos, we will update this notice and the version number on the upload screen. If the change is significant, we will ask for your permission again.

## Questions for the solicitor

1. **Basis for under-18s.** Is contract the right lawful basis for analysing an under-18's upload, given a minor's capacity to contract? Or should it be legitimate interests?
2. **Who must agree.** Is "the batter, or the batter's parent or guardian" the right person to give consent when the account holder is someone else (a parent, a coach, a friend)? Is the confirmation in clause [X].6 enough?
3. **Checking parental consent.** Is a typed name, email and tick enough for reasonable efforts under the Children's code, or must the guardian confirm by email before the clip is used?
4. **Turning 18.** When a player whose parent agreed turns 18, must we ask them to confirm, or is it enough that they can withdraw?
5. **Biometric data.** Confirm that body-pose measurements used to assess technique, never to identify anyone, are not biometric (special category) data.
6. **Reward offer.** Does "share 3 drives for a free month" keep consent freely given, provided withdrawal does not lose the reward and under-18s cannot take part?
7. **Keeping periods.** Are 3 years for clips and "video plus 1 year" for consent records reasonable? Do the 18 original clips and the Phase 4 session clips (24 months) need their own wording?
8. **Transfers.** Confirm the safeguards for Supabase, Railway, Lovable and any AI provider.
